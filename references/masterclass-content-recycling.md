# Masterclass & Content Recycling Strategy

## Why Long-Form Works in Tech/AI

1. **One-Stop Shop**: Viewers prefer one comprehensive video over searching 20 separate ones. They save it to "Watch Later" → strong algorithm signal.
2. **Massive Watch Time**: Even if only 5% watch the full 10h, 1000 people watching 1h each = 1000 hours of watch time. YouTube recommends massively.
3. **SEO Authority**: Google favors long-form. A 12h video on "Learn AI coding" is perceived as the most complete resource.

## Content Recycling Formula

1. Create individual short videos (5-15 min each) on specific topics
2. Each video earns views, subs, and revenue independently
3. Compile them into a masterclass with bridges between chapters
4. The masterclass earns additional views, subs, and revenue
5. Total effort for masterclass = ~10 minutes of editing (joining clips + adding transitions)

### Production Strategy
- Record each chapter as a standalone video first
- Publish individually for SEO on specific searches
- Once all chapters exist, compile with:
  - Chapter title cards between sections
  - A master roadmap visual showing all chapters
  - Brief bridges ("Now that you've installed Claude, let's configure it...")
  - Timestamps in description for navigation
