## Key Insights from Data

1. **Best sub conversion**: "App en 20 min" (4.5%) — showed complete transformation from zero to deployed app
2. **Best retention**: "GPT-5.4" (31.9%) — shortest video at 7:51, confirming shorter = better retention
3. **Best end viewer rate**: "GPT-5.4" (18%) — again the shortest video
4. **Best CTR**: "Scraper Sans Coder" (5.4%) — strong action-oriented title
5. **Common pattern**: All intros drop below 55%, confirming P.P.P. framework is not yet implemented

## Identified Dip Causes

- **Opening terminal without action**: Viewers leave when they see a static terminal
- **Mentioning what WON'T be covered**: Saying "I won't cover installation" causes immediate drops
- **Extended screen-only segments**: Long terminal/code segments without face cam cause steady decline

## Quick Win: Record Screen First, Voice After

The highest-potential technique for improving AVD: record the screen demo first, edit it tight with no dead time, then record voice-over on top. This eliminates all filler and gives perfect pacing control.
